PROJECT NOTES

Design overview

Project scope

A portable charger concept converting manual mechanical input into regulated electrical output through a custom circuit and enclosed prototype.

Design challenge

Provide a stable 5 V output from a variable hand-driven mechanical input and package the electrical components inside a usable enclosure.

My work

  • Developed a 13-component circuit designed to deliver a stable 5 V output.
  • Used a NEMA-17 stepper motor as the mechanical input element.
  • Applied full-bridge rectification, capacitors, and voltage regulation.
  • Designed an enclosure in Autodesk Inventor and produced it through 3D printing.
