Jigsaw Assembly

This SolidWorks jigsaw assembly combines the helical gear, eccentric pin, follower, blade guide, and motor shaft into a mechanism that converts rotary motion into the reciprocating motion needed for a jigsaw blade. Project notes

Mechanism layout

The assembly explores how a rotating drive element can actuate a follower inside a guided mechanism. In SolidWorks, the jigsaw gear, pin, follower, blade guide, and motor shaft were combined to study fit, alignment, and how the reciprocating path is constrained by the blade guide. The web viewer provides interactive inspection and a simple gear animation for presentation, but it is not a replacement for a full motion study.
