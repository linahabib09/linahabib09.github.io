PROJECT NOTES

Design overview

Project brief

Worked with a student design team on an accessible connection between Grenadier Café and Grenadier Pond, with conservation of the Black Oak Savannah as a central constraint.

Investigation

Documented steep existing trails and the longer accessible detour. Compared three alternatives: an elevated boardwalk, a gondola, and a graded switchback route.

Design outcome

The team proposed an elevated boardwalk as the preferred concept. The design report includes site analysis, alternatives and selection rationale. This was a conceptual proposal rather than a constructed installation.
