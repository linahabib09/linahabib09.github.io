PROJECT NOTES

Design overview

Project scope

A collaborative manual-transmission gearbox project modelled in SolidWorks to demonstrate the relationship between gear selection, shaft motion, and changes in output speed and torque.

Design challenge

Translate a real transmission’s mechanical principles into a clear, understandable assembly without losing the key components responsible for gear engagement.

My work

  • Contributed to the design and modelling of a three-ratio manual-transmission assembly in SolidWorks.
  • Included a dog clutch, friction clutch, and transmission shafts.
  • Organized the assembly to explain gear engagement and power transmission.
  • Designed to illustrate speed/torque variation across the gear selections.

Outcome

A team-developed educational CAD model illustrating manual transmission mechanics.
