PROJECT NOTES

Design overview

Project scope

A collaborative wearable robotics project focused on real-time tremor suppression and motion assistance for people with Parkinson’s disease. I contributed to the hardware subsystem and to mechanical concept development.

Design challenge

Develop a lightweight and compact mechanical concept intended to fit more naturally into everyday use while accommodating sensing, actuation, and control hardware.

My work

  • Collaborated with the University of Toronto NeurotechUofT hardware subsystem to co-develop a closed-loop wearable robotic-device concept comprising 20+ mechanical components.
  • Researched motors, linear actuators, and accelerometers for the hardware subsystem.
  • Co-designed the exoskeleton in SolidWorks with compactness and low weight in mind.
  • Worked within a multidisciplinary approach incorporating machine-learning algorithms and motion assistance.
