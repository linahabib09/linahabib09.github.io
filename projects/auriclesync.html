PROJECT NOTES

Design overview

Project scope

A client design project exploring improvements to audio clarity, accuracy, and synchronization for live stage performers.

Design challenge

Identify opportunities to reduce destructive wave interference and improve sound-system positioning and output under real-world venue constraints.

My work

  • Conducted site visits and audio testing, including frequency-response, SPL, and room-acoustics analysis.
  • Researched audio system placement and methods to reduce destructive interference.
  • Contributed to more than 75 ideas using 7+ ideation methods.
  • Prepared three alternative design concepts with drawings, stakeholder considerations, environmental context, and trade-off analysis for a client pitch.

Outcome

Three researched and documented design alternatives presented to the client.
