Project notes

Mechanical assembly

This project focuses on the arrangement of a motor-driven gear train and supporting shafts within a can opener mechanism. Individual gears, the motor and the assembly base were modelled and brought together in SolidWorks to study component placement and mechanical integration. The interactive 3D model is intended for visual inspection, not a demonstration of validated motion or performance. Original CAD parts and drawings are available below.

Included components

Parts and drawings

  • 8-tooth, 52-tooth, and 60-tooth gear parts
  • Supporting shafts
  • Two associated SolidWorks gear drawings
