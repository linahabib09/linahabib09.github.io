Project notes

Mechanical assembly

This project focuses on the arrangement of a motor-driven gear train and supporting shafts within a compact can opener mechanism. Individual gears, the motor, and the assembly base were modeled and then assembled in SolidWorks to study part placement, alignment, and how the mechanism would be packaged as a complete product. The interactive viewer is intended for inspection and presentation rather than validated motion analysis.

Included components

Parts and drawings

  • Assembly base and motor with integrated 8-tooth gear
  • Additional spur gears and supporting shafts
  • Compact gear-train layout for a can opener concept
