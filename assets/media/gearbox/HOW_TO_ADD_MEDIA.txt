Put your photos, PDFs or MP4 videos in this folder.
Edit media.json and add entries, e.g.:
{
  "items": [
    {"type":"image","src":"assembly.jpg","caption":"Assembly view"},
    {"type":"video","src":"demo.mp4","caption":"Mechanism demonstration"},
    {"type":"pdf","src":"drawings.pdf","caption":"Technical drawings"}
  ]
}
The first image becomes the cover image on the homepage and timeline.
